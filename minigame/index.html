RESONANCE

You wake without eyes, in a place that has forgotten light. Something here answers only to sound. Send a pulse outward and it will briefly illuminate whatever it touches - and hold weight beneath your feet, for as long as the resonance lasts.

Move - AD or ←→
Jump - W or ↑
Emit pulse - SPACE
Select frequency - 1–5
Beware spinning blades - they're deadly at any frequency.
Reach the gold beacon. Fall or crash and you'll wake at your last save point.
